# Artwork and marks

The fixed background, quiet glass surfaces and bounded glow PNGs are original
procedural SVG designs rendered offline by tools/build-artwork.cjs. Logos remain
separate transparent SVG files; no downloaded bitmap app icons or reference
photographs are included.

YouTube, Netflix, Prime Video, Twitch, Apple TV, Spotify, Plex, Crunchyroll, DAZN
and Movistar paths originate from Simple Icons 13.21.0 (CC0). Original source
URLs: https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@13.21.0/icons/NAME.svg
The complete Disney+ wordmark comes from the Disney Enterprises logo hosted at:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Disney%2B_logo.svg
https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/3e/Disney%2B_logo.svg

The stacked HBO/max artwork uses Warner Bros. Discovery paths hosted at:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:HBO_Max_2024.svg
https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/44/HBO_Max_2024.svg

Commons labels these files PD-textlogo. These brand marks are not original MIT
project artwork. HBO and max components are uniformly scaled and repositioned
to match the requested stacked proportions, with a purple max fill and theme
variants for HBO. Disney lettering changes for contrast; its arc is retained.
Original Disney SVG and adapted HBO/max paths are in tools/marks. No raster
reference images or logo backgrounds are embedded. Unknown apps use one letter.
Trademarks belong to their respective owners. No affiliation is claimed.
